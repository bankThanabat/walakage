# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who need long-running builds, downloads, uploads, presentations, or other work to continue without sleep. They use Walakage from the menu bar and need one dependable control that does not require understanding macOS power-management commands.

## Product Purpose

Walakage is a small native macOS utility for starting an Awake Session, optionally keeping the display awake, and stopping safely at a timer or battery boundary. Success means the current state is obvious, sleep prevention is trustworthy, settings stay low-friction, and the app remains fully local and unobtrusive.

## Brand Personality

Calm, native, trustworthy. Copy is concise and non-technical. The product should feel like a focused macOS control, not a monitoring dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Terminal-only power tools that expose implementation details.
- Heavy utilities with dashboards, histories, presets, or dense configuration.
- Loud destructive styling, custom themes, decorative animation, or gamified status.
- Modal-heavy workflows and confirmations for routine OFF or Quit actions.
- Interfaces that keep the display awake by default or hide battery consequences.

## Design Principles

1. Make current intent and stop reasons immediately understandable.
2. Prefer familiar native macOS controls and interaction behavior.
3. Keep safe defaults visible without turning safety into configuration clutter.
4. Stay compact and quiet while preserving exact timer and power behavior.
5. Treat accessibility as part of every control, message, and status update.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard navigation and system light and dark appearances. Label the status item `Walakage`, keep interactive VoiceOver labels aligned with visible copy, expose the shared message as status text, and make countdown text accessible without announcing every minute. Do not add color-only state, custom focus behavior, sounds, or motion that competes with the task.
