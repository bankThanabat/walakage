## Design Context

### Users

Mac users who need long-running work to continue without sleep. They use Walakage from the menu bar and expect one dependable control without needing to understand macOS power-management commands.

### Brand Personality

Calm, native, and trustworthy. Copy is concise and non-technical, and the app should feel like a focused macOS control rather than a monitoring dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

A compact native macOS utility that follows system light and dark appearances. Use familiar system controls, restrained spacing, clear alignment, and no decorative styling that competes with the task.

### Design Principles

1. Make the current intent and stop reason immediately understandable.
2. Prefer familiar native macOS controls and interaction behavior.
3. Keep safe defaults visible without adding configuration clutter.
4. Stay compact and quiet while preserving precise timer and power behavior.
5. Treat accessibility as part of every control, message, and status update.
